# Code of conduct

This project is a place to learn and to improve shared code. Everyone
who takes part, in issues, pull requests, or discussions, is expected
to help keep it useful and welcoming.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are learning.
- Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Give evidence: what you ran, what you saw.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming otherwise.
- Accept that a maintainer may decline a change, and say why when you
  decline one.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or demeaning comments about any person or group.
- Sexualized language or imagery.
- Threats, or encouraging anyone to harm themselves or others.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues or pull
requests, and block participants who break this code. Report problems
privately to the maintainer through GitHub, or by the contact details
on the author's website, imarslan.net. Reports are kept confidential.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces and when someone represents
the project elsewhere.
