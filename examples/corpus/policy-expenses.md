# Expenses

## Meals

The daily meal allowance during business travel is 60 euros, with receipts.

## Claims

Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days using form EX-12.

| Item | Limit |
|------|-------|
| Hotel per night | 180 euros |
| Taxi per trip | 40 euros |
