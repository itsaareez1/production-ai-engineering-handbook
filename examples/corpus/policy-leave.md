# Annual leave

Full-time employees receive twenty-five days of annual leave per year.

## Carry-over

Up to five days of unused annual leave may be carried over to the next year with manager approval.

Carried-over days must be used by the end of March of the following year.

## Parental leave and carry-over

Days not taken because of parental leave may all be carried over, without the five-day limit.
